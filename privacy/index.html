Privacy Policy

This policy describes Your Next Cup version 1 as it works on your iPhone. It covers what the app does. It does not describe everything Apple may do when you use iOS, Calendar, Maps, notifications, or Live Activities.

Who is responsible

Your Next Cup is made by Justin Matthew Strickler.

Privacy and support questions: jmsdesignnyc@gmail.com

No account and no developer backend

Your Next Cup does not ask you to create an account. Version 1 does not send your calendar, location, Coffee Plans, or notification contents to a server operated by the developer.

Calendar

After you allow calendar access, Your Next Cup uses Apple’s EventKit to read calendars that are already available through the iPhone Calendar system. The app uses that information on the device to show appointments and to help decide whether a coffee stop can fit.

Your Next Cup does not connect directly to Google Calendar or to Microsoft Graph. A Google, Outlook.com, or Microsoft 365 calendar is visible to the app only when that account is already available in the iPhone Calendar app. Signing in only to the separate Google Calendar or Outlook app is not enough.

The iOS permission grants calendar access at the system level. It is not a per-calendar choice. Your Next Cup’s own calendar selection then controls which of the available calendars the app considers. The app stores the identifiers of the calendars you select, on the device.

Location

Your Next Cup may ask for Location access while you are using the app. That is When In Use access. The app uses a current location reading, while it is open and in use, to:

  • set the starting point for a before-coffee time check
  • offer a suggestion when you appear to be near the café or appointment on an Active Coffee Plan

The app does not request Always Location. It does not track your location in the background. It does not keep a location history.

You can search for and select a starting place instead. That search uses Apple MapKit. A starting place you choose for the current Check Time can be kept with a Coffee Plan you save, as the plan’s origin name and coordinate. That saved origin is part of the plan. It is not a running log of where you have been.

If iOS reports an approximate location, the app can use that reading and show that it is approximate. You can still enter a starting place yourself.

Apple MapKit and Apple Maps

Your Next Cup uses Apple MapKit on the device to:

  • resolve an appointment’s location
  • search for nearby cafés
  • resolve a starting place you type
  • estimate travel time for the check you request
  • open a selected place in Apple Maps

Those requests are handled by Apple’s services. Apple’s own privacy practices apply to them. Your Next Cup does not operate a separate maps service, and the developer does not receive a copy of those MapKit requests on a developer server.

Coffee Plans

You can save one Coffee Plan on the iPhone. The saved plan can include the appointment details the plan needs, the café you chose, an origin name and coordinate, travel estimates, the coffee duration, the itinerary, and the plan’s lifecycle state.

That information is stored locally so the plan can be reviewed, started, updated, and shown again. Removing the plan removes that saved plan from the app.

Notifications

After you save an eligible Coffee Plan, Your Next Cup may explain Next Up and then ask for notification permission. If you allow notifications, Next Up can schedule one local notification from the saved plan’s leave-by time.

The notification text comes from the saved plan. The app does not keep watching traffic or your location while it is closed. If you decline notifications, the saved plan can still be used. After the system has denied notification permission, the app does not ask for that permission again. You can turn notifications on later in iOS Settings.

Live Activities

An active Coffee Plan may be shown as a Live Activity, including on the Lock Screen and, on iPhones that have it, in the Dynamic Island. The Live Activity receives the Coffee Plan’s state so it can show the current step.

The Live Activity does not independently read your location, your calendars, or MapKit.

What is stored on the device

Your Next Cup stores information it needs to function, including:

  • the identifiers of the calendars you selected
  • one saved Coffee Plan
  • limited Next Up scheduling details, such as which appointment the reminder belongs to and when it is set to fire
  • application preferences needed to run the app

Your Next Cup’s persisted app data is stored on your iPhone. Version 1 does not sync it to a developer account. Apple system services may separately process information when the app calls those services, as explained in Apple’s system services.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Version 1 does not include developer analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking. This website also does not use analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts.

Sale of personal information

Justin Matthew Strickler does not sell personal information collected by Your Next Cup.

Apple’s system services

Calendar, location, MapKit, Apple Maps, notifications, and Live Activities are Apple system services. When the app uses them, information needed for that service is handled by Apple under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. This policy does not claim that Apple collects nothing, and it does not control Apple’s practices.

Children

Your Next Cup is a general productivity and lifestyle utility for fitting a coffee stop around calendar appointments. It is not directed specifically to children, and it is not designed to collect information from children.
