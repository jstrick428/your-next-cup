My calendar isn’t showing

Your Next Cup reads calendars that the iPhone Calendar app can already see. In Your Next Cup, open Calendars and confirm the calendar is turned on.

If the app says calendar access is off, choose Full Access in Settings, then Apps, then Your Next Cup, then Calendars. You can also open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Calendars, then Your Next Cup, and choose Full Access.

If access is restricted by Screen Time or device management, the permission switch may not be available. Your Next Cup cannot override that restriction.

If the iPhone has no calendars, add an account in Settings, Apps, Calendar, Calendar Accounts, and turn Calendars on for that account. More detail is in the User Guide.

Google calendar isn’t showing

Signing in to the Google Calendar app is not enough. Add the Google account to the iPhone Calendar app and enable Calendars:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Apps, then Calendar. On older iOS versions, open Calendar from the main Settings list.
  3. Open Calendar Accounts and add the Google account if it is missing.
  4. Turn Calendars on for that account.
  5. Return to Your Next Cup and include that calendar.

Confirm the events also appear in Apple’s Calendar app. If they do not, Your Next Cup cannot see them either.

Outlook/work calendar isn’t showing

Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 use the same requirement. The account must be enabled for Calendar in the iPhone Calendar app. The Outlook app by itself does not supply events to Your Next Cup.

Use Settings, Apps, Calendar, Calendar Accounts. If the organization blocks the account, disables calendar for native apps, or manages the iPhone so the account cannot be added, Your Next Cup cannot read that calendar. Support cannot turn an employer restriction off.

No appointments today

“No appointments today” means none of the selected calendars have an event overlapping today. Check that the right calendars are selected, and that the event is on today’s date in the iPhone Calendar app. An event on another day stays off the Today list.

Appointment has no location

A café search needs a location on the event. Add a place to the event in the calendar that owns it, then open the appointment again in Your Next Cup. If the words in the location field cannot be resolved on the map, the app says it could not find that place.

Location permission denied

Before coffee cannot use your current location until access is allowed. After coffee can still be checked, and you can search for and select a starting place without granting location access.

To allow access: Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Your Next Cup, and choose While Using the App. Location Services must also be on for the iPhone. The app does not request Always.

Location restricted

Location restricted is different from a denied permission. Screen Time or device management is blocking location on the iPhone. Search for and select a starting place to check before coffee. The Enter starting place button opens that search. Turning the app’s location switch on, when that switch is even available, may not remove the restriction.

Approximate location

If the iPhone is set to Precise Location off for Your Next Cup, Check Time can use the approximate reading and labels it as approximate. For a specific origin, tap Enter starting place and choose a resolved suggestion. Precise Location, when you want it, is under Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Your Next Cup.

Enter a starting place manually

On Check Time, tap Enter starting place. Search for a place or address, then tap one of the results. Change replaces that choice. The text you type is used only after you pick a result.

No cafés found

The app asks Apple Maps for cafés within about 5 kilometers of the appointment and shows up to five of the nearest. No cafés found means MapKit returned none in that area, or none remained after that distance limit. Try an appointment whose location is in a busier area, or confirm the event location is the place you intended.

Route unavailable

Travel times come from Apple MapKit for the check you run. If a route cannot be estimated, that leg cannot be timed. Check that the appointment location and the starting place both resolve to real places, then use Refresh Travel Times or run Check Time again while you are online. MapKit availability is controlled by Apple, not by a Your Next Cup server.

Coffee no longer safely fits

When the stop no longer fits, the plan says: “This coffee stop no longer safely fits.” You can continue to the appointment. If a shorter stop still fits, the plan can offer that shorter length. Changing the duration or the café starts a new check.

Notifications are off

Next Up reminders require notification permission. The saved Coffee Plan still works without them. The app does not show the system permission prompt again after it has been denied.

To allow reminders later: Settings, Notifications, Your Next Cup, and turn Allow Notifications on. You can also use Open Settings from the notice in the app. That button opens this app’s page in Settings. It does not ask for permission a second time by itself.

Next Up didn’t arrive

Next Up is one local notification for a saved, inactive, before-coffee plan. It fires 10 minutes before the leave-by time stored on that plan. It is not sent when:

  • notifications are off, or you chose Not now
  • the plan is after coffee only
  • the plan has no usable origin
  • the plan no longer fits, or the leave-by time has passed
  • the plan is already active
  • the fire time was already in the past when the plan was saved

The reminder uses the saved plan. It does not check live traffic or your location while Your Next Cup is closed. Scheduled alerts can also be held by a Focus or by the iPhone’s notification settings.

See Next Up in the User Guide.

Live Activity isn’t visible

A Live Activity is shown for an active Coffee Plan. A saved plan that has not been started does not start one. Confirm Live Activities are allowed in Settings, then Apps, then Your Next Cup, then Live Activities, and that a Focus is not hiding them. The Live Activity shows the plan state. It does not read location or calendars on its own.

Dynamic Island isn’t visible

The Dynamic Island presentation requires an iPhone model that has a Dynamic Island, plus an active Live Activity. Other iPhones can show the Live Activity on the Lock Screen instead. There is no separate Dynamic Island switch inside Your Next Cup.

Apple Maps doesn’t open as expected

Opening a café uses Apple Maps with the place name and coordinate from the result you selected. If Maps opens a different pin, the place returned by MapKit may not be the shop you expected. Choose another result, or open that place from Maps directly. Your Next Cup does not embed its own map.
